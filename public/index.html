📱 SmartTag2 Setup Guide

1. Open SmartThings app on your Samsung phone

2. Tap your SmartTag2 → ⚙ Settings → Button actions

3. Set Press or Double press → Open link

4. Paste this URL:

https://alexa-mine.onrender.com/tap/auto

This auto-detects the time of day:
5am–12pm → Morning  |  12–5pm → Afternoon  |  5pm–5am → Night

Or use specific URLs: /tap/morning   /tap/afternoon   /tap/night
